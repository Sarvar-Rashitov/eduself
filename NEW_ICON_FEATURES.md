### O'lchamlar:
```

32x32    - Favicon (o'rtacha)

144x144  - Windows Tiles

192x192  - Android (standard + maskable)
384x384  - Android (katta)
512x512  - Splash/Install (standard + maskable)
```

- **Background**: Radial gradient (to'liq)
- **Padding**: 12.5% har tomondan
- **Safe Zone**: 75% markaziy hudud
- **Adaptive**: Har qanday shakl (doira, kvadrat, squircle)
